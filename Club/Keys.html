Clubhouse Key Information

Everything you need to know about requesting, managing and using keys for our clubhouse facilities

Getting Started

If as a club member (of either the cricket or hockey clubs) you require regular access to the clubhouse, then you are able to request a key to enable access to the appropriate areas. Keys can be requested through the Key Request Form.

Terms & Responsibilities

Key Usage Agreement

By requesting and owning a key, you should always adhere to the Key Usage Agreement, noting the guidance on what process to follow if you either lose your key, or no longer need access.

Responsibilities

Please remember that if you require the bar to be open then you responsible for providing someone to run the bar for the duration of your usage. These people need to be at least 18 years old and have received the bar/till induction/training.

If you are responsible for closing up the clubhouse and/or bar after your usage, then you must ensure that all Bar Closing and Clubhouse Locking-Up procedures are followed. They are clearly displayed by the bar door and on the front door, this includes removing rubbish to the council compound (a key for the compoiund is in each club's key lockbox in the pool room).
